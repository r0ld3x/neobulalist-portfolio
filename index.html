Who am I?

I'm Rahul — a B.Tech student specializing in Data Science who builds technology that solves meaningful problems. Programming is more than writing code to me: it's a tool for building practical things that improve people's lives.

In practice that means working across Go, Python and TypeScript and taking projects end to end — architecture, backend, interface, deployment. My work runs in production today, including an internal management system used daily by an organisation that cannot afford downtime, and a public-facing site for a consultancy.

Beyond the technical side, I'm interested in leadership, systems thinking and philosophy. Philosophy is what keeps me questioning assumptions, thinking critically, and staying pointed at long-term purpose rather than short-term wins. I enjoy breaking complex challenges into problems I can actually solve, and I learn by building, experimenting and collaborating.

HOW I_BUILD

STEP 01

Understand the actual problem

Before any code: who uses this, what they do today, and what "working" means to them. I map the real workflow first — the software comes second. That is what separates a system people adopt from one they route around.

STEP 02

Design the system, then the screens

Data model, access rules, failure modes and the 10x question get settled up front. Trade-offs get written down so nobody is surprised in month three.

STEP 03

Ship in usable slices

You get something real to click on early, not a six-month silence. Each slice is deployed, tested against actual use, and corrected before the next one starts.

STEP 04

Harden and hand over

Performance, security, logging and the boring operational details — then documentation and a path for whoever maintains it next. Including you.
